1. What SUCCESSION is

SUCCESSION is a record-keeping tool that lets jewellers, karigars and assayers keep one shared, time-stamped account of gold given, worked, returned and tested. It is not a bank, a payment service, an escrow agent or a legal arbiter.

2. Accounts

You must give accurate details and keep your password private. You are responsible for entries made from your account. Tell us promptly if you believe it has been used without permission.

3. Records and accuracy

Entries reflect what the parties recorded. SUCCESSION does not weigh or test gold and cannot confirm that a figure is correct; it makes sure that each party sees the same figure and that changes leave a trace. A recorded entry is not overwritten. Corrections are added as new entries.

4. Differences and disputes

When two readings differ by more than the agreed tolerance, the entry is flagged to both parties, who may add notes and evidence. SUCCESSION provides the timeline; the parties settle the matter between themselves or through whatever process they have agreed, and the platform takes no side.

5. Acceptable use

Do not enter false records, impersonate another person, attempt to access records you are not a party to, or interfere with the service.

6. Liability

To the extent the law allows, SUCCESSION is provided as is and is not liable for losses arising from figures entered by users or from decisions made on them. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

7. Changes

If these terms change in a material way, we will tell account holders before the change takes effect.

8. This demonstration

Dashboards on this site are a demonstration with sample data and no real accounts. Figures and names shown are illustrative.
