Privacy Policy

Version 0.9 · Effective 18 November 2026

This is a working draft prepared for the SUCCESSION demonstration. It describes how the product is designed to handle information and must be reviewed by qualified counsel before real accounts are opened.

1. What we collect

Account details: name, business or workshop name, email address, mobile number, role (jeweller, karigar or assayer) and a password, which is stored only as a salted hash.

Transaction records: weights, purity, item descriptions, timestamps, the party who made each entry, notes, acknowledgements, disputes and assay results.

Attachments you add: photographs of items, scale displays or slips.

Technical data: basic log information (time, IP address, browser type) used to keep the service secure.

2. How we use it

To operate the shared record between the parties to a transaction, to show each party the entries they are entitled to see, to calculate balances, to keep an audit trail, and to protect accounts from misuse. We do not sell personal data and do not use records for advertising.

3. Who can see a record

An entry is visible to the parties named on it: the jeweller who issued it, the karigar who received it and, where requested, the assayer asked to test it. Other users cannot see it. Support staff may access a record only to resolve a problem you report.

4. Evidence and attachments

Attachments are stored against the entry they belong to, with the time and the name of the person who added them. They cannot be edited after upload; they can only be superseded by a new, dated attachment.

5. Voice and assisted entry

Voice entry and slip reading are optional. When you use them, the audio or image is processed only to prepare a draft entry that you review and confirm. Nothing is saved to the record until you confirm it.

6. Retention

Records are kept for as long as the account is active and for the period required by applicable law for trade and tax records afterwards. Account details can be removed on request, subject to those obligations.

7. Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or export your account details and records you are a party to, and to close your account. Corrections to a transaction are made by adding a new entry, never by overwriting the old one, so that both parties keep an accurate history.

8. This demonstration

The dashboards on this site use sample data kept in your browser's local storage and a session marker in session storage. No account is created on a server, no data leaves your device, and you can clear it at any time with “Reset demo data” in the dashboard or by clearing site data in your browser.

9. Contact
