# Team-Collected Jewellery Invoices Protocol

This folder is designated for real, anonymized jewellery purchase bills and karigar job-work slips collected directly from team transactions.

## Collection Protocol
1. **Consent:** Only use invoices where the purchaser has provided written permission for evaluation use.
2. **Anonymization (Mandatory):**
   * Redact customer full name, contact numbers, email address, physical delivery address.
   * Redact payment card numbers, UPI handles, and bank account details.
   * Preserve shop name/location (if public), item description, gross weight, net weight, purity/karat, making charge, and GST rate.
3. **Storage:**
   * Place image scan or clear photograph in `images/<invoice_id>.jpg`.
   * Record ground-truth labels in `annotations.json` conforming to `annotations.schema.json`.

> **Status:** Empty. Per user instructions, no synthetic or fabricated invoices are permitted here. Real samples will be populated by the team during physical shop visits.
